Ghost Lights

Little lost ghosts drift across the marsh tonight. Carry your lantern close and they follow its light. Lead them home.

  • WASD or the arrow keys, or hold the mouse button where you want to go: walk.
  • Shift: hurry. Hurrying frightens the ghosts in your light, and they scatter.
  • Space: flare the lantern. It chases will-o'-the-wisps away and calls nearby ghosts to you. It takes a few seconds to come back.
  • R: start the night again. M: sound. Enter: continue.
  • Drag anywhere to walk. Push the stick all the way out to hurry, but hurrying frightens the ghosts and they scatter.
  • Flare chases will-o'-the-wisps away and calls nearby ghosts to you. It takes a few seconds to come back.
  • ↻ starts the night again; ♫ turns the sound off and on.

The faint ring on the ground is how far your light reaches. Fog banks swallow it, so walk close in the fog.
